The challenge

The signals that matter are buried in the noise.

Every hour, thousands of articles, broadcasts and social media posts shape how your institution is seen.

Stories break in minutes

By the time a story reaches your desk, it has often already spread across outlets and networks.

Narratives shift overnight

Tone and framing change fast. Without a daily view, emerging issues go unnoticed until they escalate.

Sources are scattered

News sites, social networks, TV and radio each live in a different place, and so does your team's work.

The question is not whether the information exists. It is whether you can see what matters, in time.

The platform

One secure platform. Every source. One clear view.

From the very first screen, your team sees the key figures of the day, the latest stories and any unusual activity, so they can act before it escalates.
